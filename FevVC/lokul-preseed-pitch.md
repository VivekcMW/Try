# lokul.club — Pre-Seed Investment Pitch

---

## The Problem

**India's neighborhoods are invisible online.**

600 million Indians live in dense, connected communities — apartment societies, mohallas, market lanes, colony gates. Yet there is no trusted digital layer for these communities. What exists today is chaos:

- **WhatsApp groups** — unmoderated, unverified, information buried in noise
- **Facebook groups** — algorithmically diluted, aging demographic, no local commerce rails
- **Sulekha / NoBroker / JustDial** — transactional directories, not communities

A resident in Koramangala doesn't know about the tuition class two buildings away. A vegetable vendor in Bhopal has no way to reach 500 households within 500 meters. A building secretary in Pune has no tool to run polls, share notices, or vet a plumber.

**The local graph exists. It just has no operating system.**

---

## The Solution

**lokul.club** is India's hyperlocal community platform — a verified, structured OS for neighborhoods across Tier 1, 2, and 3 cities.

Every locality gets its own **lokul** — a gated, geo-verified community where residents, local businesses, and service providers interact through:

- **Notice boards & local news** — structured, moderated feeds replacing WhatsApp chaos
- **Verified classifieds & local services** — discovery with trust signals, not anonymous listings
- **Community commerce** — transact locally: book a tiffin, hire a plumber, buy from your lane's kirana
- **Events & groups** — hyperlocal interest groups, society events, RWA communications

Unlike WhatsApp, membership is geo-verified. Unlike Facebook, the product is designed for locality-scale — not global broadcast. Unlike Sulekha, we build the community first, then monetize the transaction.

## Why Now

Three tailwinds have converged:

1. **UPI & Digital payments** — local commerce is now transactable without cash friction
2. **Smartphone penetration in Tier 2/3** — the next 300M users are local-first, not global
3. **Post-COVID trust deficit** — residents want vetted, known-community interactions, not strangers from the internet

WhatsApp and Facebook were built for the global social graph. **Local communities need a purpose-built product** — and that window is open.

## Business Model

**Transactional marketplace fees** on local commerce within the lokul network:

- **Service bookings** — 10–15% commission on plumbers, tutors, cleaners, cooks sourced through lokul
- **Local vendor listings + promoted slots** — kirana owners and food vendors pay to surface to their building catchment
- **Society/RWA subscriptions** — ₹2,000–5,000/month for premium community tools (polls, visitor management, maintenance tracking)

**Unit economics target (per active lokul, 12 months):**
- 200 active households → 40 monthly transactions → ₹8,000 GMV/month → ₹1,000 net revenue/lokul/month
- Path to 10,000 lokuls = ₹1 Cr/month revenue run rate
