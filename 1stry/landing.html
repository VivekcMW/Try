Frequently asked questions

Is Lokul free for residents?

Yes, Lokul is completely free for residents — always. We generate revenue from verified local businesses who pay a small fee to reach their immediate neighborhood. Residents never pay, never see ads, and never have their data sold.

How do you verify that I'm a real resident?

We use a combination of mobile OTP, address verification, and optional Aadhaar-based eKYC (coming Q3 2025). Your real identity is never exposed to other users — you can post fully anonymously. Verification only prevents fake accounts.

What's the difference between my "locality" and my city?

Your locality is defined by your 6-digit pin code, which typically covers 0.5–2 km of your home. Most pin codes in India represent a single neighborhood or sector. Your feed only shows posts from people within the same pin code — not the entire city.

Can local businesses join for free?

Yes, businesses can create a free basic listing visible to neighbors. Promoted placement in the feed and advanced analytics are available on our merchant plan (₹499/month). Early access merchants get 6 months free.

Which cities are launching first?

Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and Bengaluru are our launch cities in Q2 2025. Hyderabad, Chennai, and Pune follow in Q3. We're targeting 20 cities by end of 2025. Enter your pin code in the waitlist form and we'll notify you for your specific area.

How is Lokul different from RWA apps I already have?

Most RWA apps only cover your building or society. Lokul covers your entire pin code — connecting you to all neighbors across multiple buildings, standalone houses, and streets. It also has commercial listings, safety alerts, and a social feed that RWA-only apps don't.
