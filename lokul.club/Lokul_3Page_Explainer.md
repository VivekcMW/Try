## HOW PAYMENT WORKS

1. **Customer orders** via Lokul app (service, product, or food)
2. **Payment goes to Lokul escrow** (not directly to merchant)
3. **Service is delivered** by merchant
4. **Customer confirms receipt** in app
5. **Payment released to merchant** from escrow
6. **Trust score updates** for both parties
7. **Transaction recorded** permanently

### Benefits of This System:
- ✅ Buyer protection (can dispute if service not received)
- ✅ Seller confidence (guaranteed payment after service)
- ✅ Transaction history (builds reputation)
- ✅ Digital trails (no cash, more transparent)
- ✅ Trust signals (ratings help others decide)

## APPENDIX: COMMONLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Q: How is Lokul different from Blinkit?
**A:** Blinkit is supply-first (company owns the storefront). Lokul is merchant-first (merchant keeps the customer). Blinkit takes 40% margin; Lokul takes 5-10%. Blinkit is about speed; Lokul is about community.

### Q: Why geo-verification?
**A:** Stronger trust signals. You know the person is actually your neighbor. No fake profiles. No spam. Community identity is local-specific and defensible.

### Q: What if someone joins with a fake Aadhaar?
**A:** Aadhaar verification is unique to India—fake Aadhaar is nearly impossible. Plus, community members can report. Multiple layers of trust catch bad actors.

### Q: Why 100-300 households?
**A:** Below 100, not enough service providers. Above 300, people stop knowing each other personally. 150-200 is the sweet spot where "neighbor" still means something.

### Q: How do you make money?
**A:** Lokul takes 5-10% commission on service transactions (different from Blinkit's 40%). Also potential for merchant subscriptions, RWA partnerships, etc.

### Q: Can you operate in Tier 1 metros?
**A:** Yes, but the moat is weaker in large metros. Lokul's best advantage is Tier 2/3 where community identity is stronger and alternatives don't exist.

### Q: What happens if someone leaves negative reviews?
**A:** Reputation is based on verified transactions, not anonymous reviews. Community leaders can verify if complaint is legitimate. Disputes are resolved fairly.

### Q: Is Lokul only for buying/selling?
**A:** No. Lokul is a full community platform: notices, events, disputes, safety alerts, community help, etc. Commerce is just one layer.
