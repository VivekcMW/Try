# Lokul.club — Executive Summary for Investors

**Date:** July 27, 2026  
**Assessment Grade:** A- (Strong concept, needs micro-scale proof)

---

## 🎯 THE OPPORTUNITY

**Market:** India's ₹250B+ unplatformed neighborhood economy  
**Solution:** Radius-based hyperlocal platform enabling P2P services + local business discovery  
**Unique Insight:** Trust comes from proximity — neighbors hiring neighbors, not strangers from the internet

---

## ✅ KEY STRENGTHS

1. **Bottom-up community creation** — NOT dependent on RWA gatekeepers
2. **Role fluidity** — every user is buyer + seller (Cook, Rider, Coach, Reseller roles)
3. **Radius as primitive** (200m/500m/2km) — matches how Indians think about locality
4. **Trust tier architecture** (Bronze/Silver/Gold KYC) — India-compliant by design
5. **Market timing** — UPI + smartphone penetration + post-COVID trust window
6. **90% addressable** — works in societies, lanes, mohallas, colonies (not just gated apartments)

---

## ⚠️ KEY RISKS

1. **P2P services at scale UNPROVEN** — will Indians hire neighbors vs. Swiggy/Urban Company?
2. **Solo founder** — ops-heavy model needs co-founder/founding operator
3. **Zero pilot data** — no proof ONE radius actually works with real transactions
4. **Content moderation** — discrimination, safety incidents in open communities
5. **Informal economy friction** — supply may not want digital visibility/taxes
6. **Unit economics unclear** — path from 1 radius → 10,000 radii with $750K unrealistic
