# Lokul.club — Investor Due Diligence Checklist

**Purpose:** Questions you MUST answer before your next investor meeting  
**Date:** July 27, 2026

---

## 📋 TEAM & EXECUTION

### Co-Founder / Team Questions

- [ ] **Do you have a co-founder?** If yes, name and background?
- [ ] **If solo, who is your founding operator/first hire?** Have they committed?
- [ ] **What's your co-founder equity split?** (If applicable)
- [ ] **Do you have an advisory board?** Names and domains?
- [ ] **Who covers operations while you code?** (Or vice versa)
- [ ] **What happens if you get sick for 2 months?** Who runs the company?
- [ ] **Why don't you have a co-founder yet?** (Investors will ask this)

**Investor Red Flags:**
- ❌ "Haven't found the right person yet" (signals: been looking, failing)
- ❌ "I work better alone" (signals: poor judgment, not coachable)
- ❌ "I'll hire after funding" (signals: no urgency, low commitment from others)

**Good Answers:**
- ✅ "Solo by choice for now; have identified [Name] with [background], in final discussions"
- ✅ "First hire is founding operator with co-founder equity path over 12 months"
- ✅ "Advisory board: [3 names with specific domains they help with]"

## 📊 TRACTION & PROOF

### Pilot Geography

- [ ] **What is your ONE pilot geography?** (Specific: "XYZ Society, Andheri" not "Mumbai")
- [ ] **How many users have you onboarded?** (Real numbers, not projections)
- [ ] **How did you recruit them?** (Door-to-door? RWA blast? Manual outreach?)
- [ ] **What's your retention?** (D7, D30, D90 with cohort data)

### Transactions & GMV

- [ ] **How much GMV have you generated?** (₹ amount, time period)
- [ ] **How many transactions?** (Count, across how many users)
- [ ] **What categories are transacting?** (Cook? Rider? Local merchant? Classifieds?)
- [ ] **What's your average order value?** (AOV)
- [ ] **What's your take rate?** (% you keep per transaction)

### Supply Side

- [ ] **How many peer providers are active?** (Cook, Rider, Coach, etc.)
- [ ] **Are they earning real money?** (₹ amounts per month per provider)
- [ ] **Why did they join Lokul?** (vs. continuing offline/WhatsApp-only)
- [ ] **Are they exclusive or multi-homing?** (Using Lokul + Swiggy + WhatsApp?)

### Product Usage

- [ ] **What's your DAU/MAU (stickiness)?**
- [ ] **How many posts/day in your pilot radius?**
- [ ] **What's your NPS?** (Net Promoter Score from users)
- [ ] **Top 3 features being used?** (Feed? Services? Chat? Events?)
- [ ] **Top 3 user complaints?** (What's broken or missing?)

**If You Have No Data Yet:**
- ✅ Be honest: "We haven't launched pilot yet; raising to prove first radius"
- ✅ Share plan: "90-day plan to activate [specific geography] with [specific approach]"
- ❌ Don't make up numbers or say "we're about to launch" repeatedly

## 💰 UNIT ECONOMICS

### Customer Acquisition

- [ ] **What's your CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)?**
  - Per household/resident: ₹___
  - Per radius activation: ₹___
- [ ] **How are you acquiring users?** (Paid ads? RWA partnerships? Referrals? Door-to-door?)
- [ ] **What's your payback period?** (How many months to recover CAC from user LTV?)

### Lifetime Value

- [ ] **What's your LTV (Lifetime Value)?**
  - Per household over 24 months: ₹___
- [ ] **What's your LTV:CAC ratio?** (Target: >8:1 for healthy marketplace)
- [ ] **What assumptions are you making?** (Transactions/month, retention, take rate)

### Path to Profitability

- [ ] **When does one radius become cash-flow positive?** (Month 3? Month 6?)
- [ ] **What's your burn rate?** (₹/month with current team + ops)
- [ ] **How long does $750K last?** (Runway in months)
- [ ] **What metrics do you hit with $750K?** (Be specific: X radii, Y users, Z GMV)

## 🚀 GO-TO-MARKET

### Activation Playbook

- [ ] **How do you activate ONE radius?** (Step-by-step SOP)
  1. _______________
  2. _______________
  3. _______________

- [ ] **How long does activation take?** (Weeks from first contact to 50 active users)
- [ ] **What's your success rate?** (Out of 10 attempted radii, how many become active?)
- [ ] **What role do community managers play?** (What do they actually DO daily?)

### Scaling Plan

- [ ] **How do you go from 1 radius → 10 radii?**
- [ ] **How do you go from 10 radii → 100 radii?**
- [ ] **How many community managers do you need per 50 radii?**
- [ ] **What's your hiring plan?** (Timeline, comp structure, locations)

### Geography Strategy

- [ ] **Which city are you starting in?** Why that city?
- [ ] **Are you launching in metros or Tier 2/3?** Why?
- [ ] **When do you expand to second city?** (After how many radii in city 1?)
- [ ] **Which languages are critical for launch?** (English + Hindi enough, or need regional?)

## ⚖️ COMPETITIVE & MARKET

### Why Past Failures Don't Apply

- [ ] **Why did LocalCircles fail to monetize?** (And why won't you?)
- [ ] **Why did Nextdoor struggle in India?** (And why will you succeed?)
- [ ] **What's different about your approach?** (Specific, not "better execution")

### Competitive Threats

- [ ] **What if Swiggy adds a "hyperlocal peer cook" feature?**
- [ ] **What if Urban Company adds a "trusted neighbor" network?**
- [ ] **What if WhatsApp adds commerce/payments in groups?**
- [ ] **What if Reliance JioMart adds community layers?**

### Why P2P Services Work

- [ ] **Why will neighbors hire neighbors vs. professional platforms?**
  - Lower price? (How much lower?)
  - Better trust? (How do you build it faster than Urban Company?)
  - More convenience? (In what way?)
  - Better quality? (How do you ensure consistency?)

- [ ] **What's the typical user scenario?**
  - Example: "Mrs. Sharma needs tiffin daily. Today she orders Swiggy (₹120/meal). Tomorrow she sees neighbor Mrs. Patel on Lokul (₹70/meal, same building). Why does she switch?"

## 🛡️ TRUST & SAFETY

### Moderation Plan

- [ ] **Who moderates content in open communities?** (Without RWA admins)
- [ ] **What's your policy on spam/scams?** (Detection, resolution SLA)
- [ ] **What's your AI moderation accuracy?** (False positive rate, false negative rate)

### Safety Incidents

- [ ] **What happens when peer service goes wrong?**
  - Food poisoning from peer cook?
  - Theft by peer handyman?
  - Accident during peer carpool?

- [ ] **Who is liable?** (Provider? Platform? User?)
- [ ] **Do you have insurance?** (What coverage, which transactions?)
- [ ] **What's your incident response playbook?**

### Discrimination & Bias

- [ ] **What's your policy on caste/religion-based rejection?**
- [ ] **How do you detect discrimination?** (User reports peer cook but reason is caste, not quality)
- [ ] **What's your enforcement?** (Ban user? Warning? Mediation?)
- [ ] **Have you consulted legal + social science experts?** (Names?)

## 🧠 FOUNDER-MARKET FIT

### Personal Connection

- [ ] **Why are YOU the right person to build this?**
  - Personal experience with the problem?
  - Domain expertise in hyperlocal/marketplace?
  - Unique insight no one else has?

- [ ] **What's your unfair advantage?**
  - Network? (Access to RWAs, communities, suppliers?)
  - Experience? (Built similar product before?)
  - Insight? (Lived in the target geography, understand culture?)

### Commitment

- [ ] **Are you full-time on this?** (Or side project?)
- [ ] **How long can you bootstrap without funding?** (Personal runway)
- [ ] **What's your backup plan if this fails?** (Investors want to know you're committed but rational)

## ✅ FINAL READINESS CHECK

### Green Lights (Ready to Pitch)
- [ ] Have pilot data (or clear 90-day plan to get it)
- [ ] Co-founder identified or founding operator committed
- [ ] Unit economics modeled (even if assumptions-based)
- [ ] Clear differentiation vs. failed predecessors
- [ ] Trust & safety policy drafted
- [ ] Revised ask matches stage (not asking for seed at pre-seed)

### Yellow Lights (Need Work Before Pitching)
- [ ] Solo founder with no team plan
- [ ] No pilot geography identified
- [ ] Unit economics unclear
- [ ] "We'll figure it out after funding" answers

### Red Lights (Not Ready — Don't Pitch Yet)
- [ ] No clear problem understanding
- [ ] Copying Western models without India adaptation
- [ ] Asking for too much capital without proof
- [ ] Can't explain why past companies failed
- [ ] No personal commitment/full-time
